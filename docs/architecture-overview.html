Trust postures

Autonomous: open case, draft message, stage warranty claim. Reversible or drafts; the 72 hour entitlement window makes inaction the worse failure.
Recommend: dispatch technician, ship or preposition parts, send message, submit claim. Cost, customer impact, or financial commitment.
Human required: escalate to engineer, approve payout. Also the default for anything not in the matrix.

Where the boundaries sit

Data 360 ends at identity and grounding. It does not own the gate and does not store telemetry.
Agentforce ends at the action signature. It chooses which action to call; Apex decides whether it is allowed.
Salesforce ends at the record. Adjudication, payout, parts fulfillment, and telemetry storage stay with the customer.

Legend: built designed, not built trust gate, tested (4 of 4 Apex tests pass)
